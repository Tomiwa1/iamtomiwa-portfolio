Refund Policy

What you can get back, and when.

We work in milestones. You pay for each stage as it is agreed, you approve each stage as it is delivered, and anything not yet delivered is refundable. This page explains exactly how that works.

Summary

Approved work is final. Once you approve a milestone, that portion of the fee is earned and non-refundable — the time has been spent and the files are yours.

Unapproved work is refundable. Any milestone you have paid for but not yet approved is refunded in full if you cancel, minus any work already delivered within it.

We sell digital services, not physical goods. Nothing is shipped, so there are no returns, delivery charges or restocking fees — only refunds of money paid for work not yet completed.

How milestones work

Before any project starts, you receive a written scope that breaks the package into milestones. A typical breakdown looks like this:

  • Milestone 1 — Discovery and direction. Kick-off call, goals, structure, and initial design direction.
  • Milestone 2 — Design. Full designs for the agreed pages or screens, desktop and mobile.
  • Milestone 3 — Build and handoff. The live site or the developer-ready files, plus any SEO, CMS or analytics work in your package.

Each milestone is invoiced and paid before that stage of work begins. When a milestone is delivered you have 5 business days to review it and either approve it or send revisions within the rounds included in your package. If we don't hear from you within 5 business days, the milestone is treated as approved so the project can move on.

The number and shape of milestones varies with the package. Custom engagements and retainers are milestoned in the written scope agreed at the start.

What is refundable

  • Milestones paid for but not started. Refunded in full.
  • A milestone in progress but not yet approved. Refunded in proportion to the work not yet delivered, assessed against the written scope.
  • Duplicate or incorrect payments. Refunded in full as soon as they are identified.
  • Work we cannot deliver. If we cancel a project, or cannot complete it for any reason on our side, every unapproved milestone is refunded in full.
  • Work that does not meet the agreed brief. If a delivered milestone does not match the written scope and we cannot correct it within your included revision rounds, that milestone is refunded.

What is not refundable

  • Approved milestones. Once approved, expressly or by the 5 business day review window passing, that fee is earned.
  • Completed projects. Once final files or a live site have been delivered and approved, the project is closed.
  • Change of mind or change of direction after work on a milestone has begun — for example rebranding mid-project, or deciding not to launch.
  • Delays caused by missing input. If a project stalls because content, feedback or access hasn't been provided, the time already spent is not refundable.
  • Third-party costs already paid on your behalf — domains, hosting, fonts, stock assets, plugins or subscriptions. These are passed through at cost and are subject to the third party's own refund terms.
  • Requests for work outside the agreed scope that were quoted and paid for separately and already delivered.

Cancelling a project

You can cancel at any time by emailing us. On cancellation:

  • Work stops at the end of the current milestone.
  • Approved milestones are invoiced or retained in full.
  • The milestone in progress is assessed against the scope, and the unearned portion is refunded.
  • Milestones not yet started are refunded in full.
  • You keep everything already delivered and paid for, under the licence terms in our Terms and Conditions.

Retainers can be cancelled with 14 days' written notice. The current month is not refunded, and no further months are charged.

How to request a refund

Email tomiwa1.dev@gmail.com with:

  • Your name and the email address used when booking
  • The project or package name
  • The milestone the request relates to
  • What you'd like refunded, and why

We acknowledge refund requests within 2 business days and give you a decision, with reasons, within 7 business days. If a request is declined we will explain which part of this policy applies and what we can offer instead.

Processing and timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method — we cannot refund to a different card, account or person. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of approval.

How quickly the money reaches you after that depends on your bank or card issuer, and is outside our control. Card refunds typically settle within 5–10 business days; bank transfers are usually faster.

Refunds are issued in the currency originally paid. Where a payment was converted, any difference caused by exchange-rate movement or fees charged by your bank or card issuer is not something we can recover or reimburse.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, ask before you book — we would rather explain it upfront than argue about it later. Our contact details and hours are on the Contact Us page.

This policy sits alongside our Terms and Conditions and does not affect your statutory rights under Nigerian consumer protection law, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018.
