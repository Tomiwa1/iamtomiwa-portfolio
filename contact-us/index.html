Complaints and disputes

If something has gone wrong, email us first with the details and what outcome you're looking for. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 2 business days and to resolve them within 14 days.

If we can't reach an agreement, the dispute process is set out in section 12 of our Terms and Conditions. Raising a complaint with us directly is usually faster than a payment chargeback, and it doesn't affect any rights you have under Nigerian consumer law.
