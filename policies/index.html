Part 2

Refund Policy

Approved work is final. Once you approve a milestone, that portion of the fee is earned and non-refundable — the time has been spent and the files are yours.

Unapproved work is refundable. Any milestone you have paid for but not yet approved is refunded in full if you cancel, minus any work already delivered within it.

We sell digital services, not physical goods. Nothing is shipped, so there are no returns, delivery charges or restocking fees — only refunds of money paid for work not yet completed.

How milestones work

Before any project starts, you receive a written scope that breaks the package into milestones. A typical breakdown looks like this:

  • Milestone 1 — Discovery and direction. Kick-off call, goals, structure, and initial design direction.
  • Milestone 2 — Design. Full designs for the agreed pages or screens, desktop and mobile.
  • Milestone 3 — Build and handoff. The live site or the developer-ready files, plus any SEO, CMS or analytics work in your package.

Each milestone is invoiced and paid before that stage of work begins. When a milestone is delivered you have 5 business days to review it and either approve it or send revisions within the rounds included in your package. If we don't hear from you within 5 business days, the milestone is treated as approved so the project can move on.

What is refundable

  • Milestones paid for but not started. Refunded in full.
  • A milestone in progress but not yet approved. Refunded in proportion to the work not yet delivered, assessed against the written scope.
  • Duplicate or incorrect payments. Refunded in full as soon as they are identified.
  • Work we cannot deliver. If we cancel a project, or cannot complete it for any reason on our side, every unapproved milestone is refunded in full.
  • Work that does not meet the agreed brief. If a delivered milestone does not match the written scope and we cannot correct it within your included revision rounds, that milestone is refunded.

What is not refundable

  • Approved milestones — once approved, expressly or by the 5 business day review window passing, that fee is earned.
  • Completed projects — once final files or a live site have been delivered and approved, the project is closed.
  • Change of mind or direction after work on a milestone has begun.
  • Delays caused by missing input — if a project stalls because content, feedback or access hasn't been provided, time already spent is not refundable.
  • Third-party costs already paid on your behalf — domains, hosting, fonts, stock assets, plugins or subscriptions, which follow the third party's own refund terms.

Cancelling a project

You can cancel at any time by emailing us. Work stops at the end of the current milestone; approved milestones are retained in full; the milestone in progress is assessed against the scope and the unearned portion refunded; milestones not yet started are refunded in full. You keep everything already delivered and paid for.

Retainers can be cancelled with 14 days' written notice. The current month is not refunded, and no further months are charged.

How to request a refund

Email tomiwa1.dev@gmail.com with your name, the email address used when booking, the project or package name, the milestone concerned, and what you'd like refunded and why.

We acknowledge refund requests within 2 business days and give you a decision, with reasons, within 7 business days. If a request is declined we will explain which part of this policy applies and what we can offer instead.

Processing and timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method — we cannot refund to a different card, account or person — and are processed within 5 business days of approval. How quickly the money reaches you after that depends on your bank or card issuer; card refunds typically settle within 5–10 business days.

Refunds are issued in the currency originally paid. Where a payment was converted, differences caused by exchange-rate movement or fees charged by your bank are not something we can recover or reimburse.

This policy does not affect your statutory rights under Nigerian consumer protection law, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018.

Privacy Policy

We collect the minimum needed to quote for work, deliver it, and get paid. We never sell your data, and we never see your card details.

Who is responsible

Samuel Akinbode, a sole proprietor operating from Gbagada, Lagos State, Nigeria, is the data controller for personal data collected through iamtomiwa.xyz and in the course of client work. This policy is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), overseen by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). Where a client is based outside Nigeria, we apply the same standards to their data.

What we collect

  • Contact details — your name, email address, and phone number if you provide one.
  • Project information — the brief, goals, timelines, budgets, references and any content or assets you send us.
  • Business details — company name and billing address where needed to raise an invoice.
  • Correspondence — the emails and messages you send us, kept as a record of what was agreed.
  • Basic technical data — IP address, browser and device type, referring page and pages viewed, where analytics is enabled.

We do not collect special-category data — health, biometric, religious or political information — and we ask that you don't send it to us.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotes — steps taken at your request before entering a contract.
  • To deliver the work you have booked and hand over files — performance of our contract with you.
  • To invoice and take payment, and keep the records tax law requires — legal obligation.
  • To improve the website — legitimate interest, using aggregate data only.
  • To show work in a portfolio — only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Payments and card data

We never see or store your card details. Card numbers, CVVs and bank credentials are entered directly with our payment processor and handled on their PCI-DSS compliant systems, not ours.

What we receive back is limited to what we need to reconcile an invoice: your name, the amount, the date, the transaction reference, and whether the payment succeeded. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We share it only with providers who help us run the business, and only as far as they need it: payment processors, email and file storage providers, design and hosting tools, professional advisers, and authorities where Nigerian law requires. Some providers are based outside Nigeria, so your data may be processed abroad; where that happens we rely on providers offering protections consistent with the NDPA.

How we keep it secure

  • The website is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your browser and the site is encrypted.
  • Accounts holding client data use strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication where supported.
  • Access is limited to the business owner. No third party has standing access to client files.
  • Files no longer needed are deleted rather than archived indefinitely.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and creates a real risk to you, we will notify you and the NDPC as the NDPA requires.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become projects — up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Project files and correspondence — the life of the project plus 3 years.
  • Invoices and payment records — 6 years, as tax and accounting rules require.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you can access the data we hold, correct anything inaccurate, ask us to delete it where we have no legal reason to keep it, object to or restrict how we use it, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time.

Email tomiwa1.dev@gmail.com to exercise any of these. We respond within 30 days and do not charge for it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Cookies, analytics and children

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies and runs no third-party ad networks. Where analytics is enabled it is used only to count visits and see which pages are read, never to build a profile or target advertising. You can block cookies in your browser without losing access to any part of this site.

Our services are sold to businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with an updated date at the top. Where a change materially affects how we handle data for an active project, we will tell affected clients by email.
