Privacy Policy

What we collect, and what we don't.

We collect the minimum needed to quote for work, deliver it, and get paid. We never sell your data, and we never see your card details. This policy explains what is held, why, for how long, and how to get it removed.

1. Who is responsible

Samuel Akinbode, a sole proprietor operating from Gbagada, Lagos State, Nigeria, is the data controller for personal data collected through iamtomiwa.xyz and in the course of client work.

This policy is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), which is overseen by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). Where a client is based outside Nigeria, we apply the same standards to their data.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — your name, email address, and phone number if you provide one.
  • Project information — the brief, goals, timelines, budgets, references and any content or assets you send us for the work.
  • Business details — company name and billing address where these are needed to raise an invoice.
  • Correspondence — the emails and messages you send us, kept as a record of what was agreed.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and pages viewed, collected when analytics is enabled on the site.

We do not collect special-category data — health, biometric, religious or political information — and we ask that you don't send it to us.

3. Why we collect it

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotes — on the basis of steps taken at your request before entering a contract.
  • To deliver the work you have booked, communicate about it, and hand over files — on the basis of performing our contract with you.
  • To invoice and take payment, and to keep the records tax law requires — on the basis of legal obligation.
  • To improve the website and understand which pages are useful — on the basis of legitimate interest, using aggregate data only.
  • To show work in a portfolio — only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Confidential projects are never published, and are covered by the confidentiality terms in our Terms and Conditions.

4. Payments and card data

We never see or store your card details. Card numbers, CVVs and bank credentials are entered directly with our payment processor and are handled on their PCI-DSS compliant systems, not ours.

What we receive back from the processor is limited to what we need to reconcile an invoice: your name, the amount, the date, the transaction reference, and whether the payment succeeded. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, and only as far as they need it:

  • Payment processors — to take payment and issue refunds.
  • Email and file storage providers — to correspond with you and hold project files.
  • Design, hosting and analytics tools — to produce and deliver the work.
  • Professional advisers — accountants or lawyers, where we are required to.
  • Authorities — where disclosure is required by Nigerian law.

Some of these providers are based outside Nigeria, so your data may be processed abroad. Where that happens we rely on providers that offer protections consistent with the NDPA.

6. How we keep it secure

  • The website is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your browser and the site is encrypted.
  • Accounts holding client data are protected with strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication where the provider supports it.
  • Access is limited to the business owner. No third party is given standing access to client files.
  • Files no longer needed are deleted rather than archived indefinitely.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and creates a real risk to you, we will notify you and the NDPC as the NDPA requires.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become projects — up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Project files and correspondence — for the life of the project and 3 years afterwards, so we can support work we delivered.
  • Invoices and payment records — 6 years, as tax and accounting rules require.

You can ask us to delete your data sooner, and we will unless we are legally required to keep it.

8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your data where we have no legal reason to keep it.
  • Object to or restrict how we use it, including for portfolio use.
  • Portability — receive your data in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is what we relied on.

Email tomiwa1.dev@gmail.com to exercise any of these. We respond within 30 days and do not charge for it.

9. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not run third-party ad networks.

Where analytics is enabled, it is used only to count visits and see which pages are read. It is not used to build a profile of you or to target advertising. You can block cookies in your browser settings without losing access to any part of this site.

10. Children

Our services are sold to businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with an updated date at the top. Where a change materially affects how we handle data for an active project, we will tell affected clients by email.

12. Contact and complaints

Questions about this policy, or about data we hold, go to tomiwa1.dev@gmail.com. Full contact details and hours are on the Contact Us page.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), the supervisory authority for data protection in Nigeria.
