2. Services and scope

We provide digital design and build services — website design, product design, front-end build, SEO, design systems and related work. Packages, prices and deliverables are listed on the Services page.

Everything we deliver is digital. No physical goods are sold or shipped, and there are no delivery charges.

Package descriptions on the website are a summary. The binding scope for your project is the written scope we send and you approve before work begins, covering deliverables, milestones, timeline, revision rounds and price.

5. Milestones and approval

Projects are split into milestones set out in the written scope. When a milestone is delivered you have 5 business days to approve it or request revisions within your included rounds. If we don't hear from you in that window, the milestone is treated as approved and the project moves to the next stage.

Approval means that stage is complete and the fee for it is earned. What that means for refunds is set out in section 10 and in the Refund Policy.

6. Revisions and extra work

Each package includes a set number of revision rounds, listed on the Services page and repeated in your scope. A round means one consolidated set of feedback, not an open-ended stream of individual changes.

Requests that fall outside the agreed scope — extra pages, new features, a change of direction after approval, or additional rounds — are quoted separately and only begin once you approve the additional cost in writing.

Timelines are estimates given in good faith. They assume feedback and materials arrive when agreed, and they shift if they don't.
