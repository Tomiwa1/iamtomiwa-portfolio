Who we are

Samuel Akinbode is a product and web designer operating as a sole proprietor in Nigeria. The business is owned and run by one person — there is no separate company entity, and all contracts, invoices and refunds are issued in the owner's name.

The practice has been taking on client work since 2022, with a portfolio spanning fintech, wallets and consumer products. You can see selected work on the Works and Case Studies pages.

What we do

We sell fixed-scope design and build services. Every package is listed with its price, deliverables and timeline on the Services page:

  • Single Page Design — a one-page website, designed and shipped.
  • Full Website & SEO — a multi-page site with search optimisation and analytics.
  • Everything, Shipped — a full site plus design system, CMS and post-launch support.
  • Custom engagements — product design and ongoing design support, scoped per project.

We do not sell physical goods. Everything delivered is digital: design files, prototypes, live websites, and documentation. Because of this, nothing is shipped and there are no delivery charges.

How we work

Work is agreed in writing before it starts. You receive a scope covering deliverables, milestones, timeline and price, and payment is taken against milestones rather than as a single upfront lump sum. Full details are in our Terms and Conditions, and refund terms are set out in the Refund Policy.

Clients are kept in the loop as the work happens rather than shown one reveal at the end, so feedback lands early and nothing gets built twice.
